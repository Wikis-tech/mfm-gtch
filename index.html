Who We Are

About MFM Guitar Choir

An officially recognized music ministry under Mountain of Fire and Miracles Ministries, dedicated to spirit-filled worship and evangelism

Our Vision

To glorify God and advance the vision of MFM through disciplined, guitar-led worship that ministers deliverance, revival, and spiritual upliftment.

Our Mission

Supporting church services and MFM programs through inspired music ministry, evangelism, and training members in line with MFM standards.

Our Impact

Lagos Choir Awards winner (2020), with over a decade of consistent ministry and representation of MFM in local and international platforms.

Our Journey

Key Milestones

Celebrating God's faithfulness through the years

2006

Formal establishment of the MFM Guitar Choir at MFM International Headquarters, Lagos, Nigeria on September 3rd

Celebrated 12th Anniversary milestone with testimonies of growth and spiritual impact

Celebrating 19 years of faithful service and spirit-filled ministry
